Give your TV a quieter home.

QuietDeck TV Launcher brings your favourite apps together in a clean Android TV home screen. Navigate with your remote, find an app by name, and organize your own collections without recommendation feeds or promotional rows.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Choose and reorder favourites.
• Find installed apps with search.
• Create named collections for movies, music, games or family apps.
• Return to up to 12 apps opened through QuietDeck, or clear that list anytime.
• Hide apps from the launcher and restore them in Settings.

A CLEARER VIEW
• App banners and icons keep their original proportions.
• Choose four, five or six columns and three app-label sizes.
• Use a high-contrast background, three built-in scenes or your own local wallpaper.
• Open app options from a visible button or by holding OK.

SMALL, LOCAL AND PRIVATE
QuietDeck uses native Android controls, a bounded artwork cache and no third-party runtime libraries. It has no ads, accounts, analytics or internet permission. Your configuration and recently opened list stay on your TV. Export and restore settings through a compatible Android document picker.

MADE FOR ANDROID TV
Requires Android 6.0 or later on a TV device. Default Home selection, input settings and document-picker availability depend on your TV manufacturer. QuietDeck provides shortcuts to available system settings; it cannot bypass manufacturer Home-button restrictions. Some apps may be unavailable or unsuitable for remote use.

QuietDeck changes your launcher home screen. Streaming content and subscriptions are provided by the apps you install. QuietDeck is independent of Google, Apple, TV manufacturers and streaming services.

Free and open source: https://github.com/junaid-devv/quietdeck-tv-launcher
