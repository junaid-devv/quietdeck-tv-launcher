QuietDeck TV Launcher

Privacy policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · Developer: Muhammad Junaid

This policy covers QuietDeck TV Launcher, including the 2.0 release candidate, application ID com.still.launcher.

No off-device collection

QuietDeck requests no Android permissions, including INTERNET. It has no accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics, tracking or server connection. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your app data.

Information used on your TV

Installed app names, package identifiers and artwork are processed on your device to display and launch apps. Favourites, hidden apps, collections, appearance settings and up to 12 identifiers of apps opened through QuietDeck are stored in its private local storage. Automatic Android application backup and device transfer are excluded by the app configuration.

Your controls and retention

Clear the recently opened list in Settings. Clear application storage or uninstall QuietDeck to delete its local settings. Exported configuration files contain favourites, hidden apps, collections and appearance settings. They exclude recent activity and wallpaper. Files remain in the location you choose until you delete them. Importing a backup replaces the corresponding local settings.

Wallpapers and document providers

A wallpaper you select is read through the Android document picker. Its document address and read permission are retained locally so it can be displayed again. Replacing or removing the wallpaper releases the old read permission; the original file is unchanged. File providers may offer cloud destinations or use their own network connection. Their privacy policies apply to those services.

Other services

Apps you open follow their own privacy policies. Android and Google Play may process information under their own policies. The project website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process website access information under its privacy policy. Website visits are separate from use of the launcher.

Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, use the project issue tracker. Avoid posting personal data. For security issues, use private vulnerability reporting when available.

Changes

If these practices change, this policy, the in-app policy and the store disclosures will be updated.
