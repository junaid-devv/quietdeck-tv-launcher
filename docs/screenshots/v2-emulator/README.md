# QuietDeck 2.0.0-rc2 screenshots

These unaltered Android TV API 30 x86 emulator captures show the restored original Home design. They were captured from running native UI, not drawn as mockups or retouched.

Home uses three separately installed original, non-functional demonstration apps: Quiet Cinema (wide artwork), Quiet Music (square) and Quiet Games (tall). They are not included in the APK/AAB. Other visible apps belong to the emulator image; they are not QuietDeck integrations. Your TV shows your chosen installed apps.

- `default-*`: 1920 × 1080, 320 dpi, normal fonts.
- `720p-*`: 1280 × 720, 213 dpi, normal fonts.
- `large-text-contrast-*`: 1080p, 320 dpi, 1.3 font scale and high contrast.
- `4k-output-*`: 2160p output override with 320 dpi logical density. This TV runtime keeps a 1080p app surface; these are not native 4K-rendering captures.

The two listing screenshots are exact copies of default Home and Apps. Source: `8e00885aa991187e6b9440d33e56447e22390168`. [Capture run](https://github.com/junaid-devv/quietdeck-tv-launcher/actions/runs/39574392581). No physical TV qualification is implied.
